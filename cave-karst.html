Cave and Karst Studies

Karst landscapes — shaped by the dissolution of soluble rocks such as limestone, dolomite, and gypsum — cover roughly 15–25% of Earth's land surface and provide freshwater to more than a quarter of the global population. The caves, sinkholes, springs, and underground drainage networks that define these terrains are not only geological wonders, but critical ecosystems and archives of Earth history.

The Jones Lab, in partnership with the National Cave and Karst Research Institute (NCKRI), investigates the microbiology and geochemistry of cave and karst systems. Below you will find links to recent news, academic programs, and key organizations for anyone interested in exploring this field.

Analysis of 2,500+ bones from Baishiya Karst Cave on the Tibetan Plateau (3,280 m elevation) reveals that Denisovans and other archaic humans survived at extreme altitude for at least 160,000 years.

Key Organizations & Resources

  • National Cave and Karst Research Institute (NCKRI)

    Created by U.S. Congress in 1998 and based in Carlsbad, NM, NCKRI is the primary U.S. nexus for cave and karst research, resource management, education, and stewardship.

    nckri.org →

  • National Speleological Society (NSS)

    The world's largest cave-focused membership organization, with 8,000+ members in 250+ grottos. Founded in 1941, the NSS promotes exploration, conservation, and scientific study of caves.

    caves.org →

  • Karst Waters Institute

    A scientific organization dedicated to karst hydrology and water resources research, publishing peer-reviewed work and hosting major symposia including the Appalachian Karst Symposium.

    karstwaters.org →

  • Journal of Cave and Karst Studies (JCKS)

    The flagship peer-reviewed journal for speleological research across geology, hydrology, biology, archaeology, and paleontology. Published by the NSS.

    caves.org/publications/journal →

  • International Journal of Speleology (IJS)

    An open-access journal affiliated with the Union Internationale de Spéléologie, publishing peer-reviewed research on karst and cave sciences worldwide.

    digitalcommons.usf.edu/ijs →

  • IAH Karst Commission

    The karst-focused commission of the International Association of Hydrogeologists, advancing research and best practices in karst aquifer characterization and management.

    iah.org →
